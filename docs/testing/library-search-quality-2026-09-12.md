## Corpus and oracle

`server/internal/testutil/search_quality_cases.go` declares each query's label,
category, metric, and acceptable **typed identity set before execution**. Cases
are literal product examples and catalog-based intents, not generated by the
normalizer or copied from observed HTTP output. Ambiguous `Twin`, `Same Coin`,
recording/version, and broad-word intents permit alternative identities; a
unique full-name intent has exactly one target. Existing exact ordering oracles
remain authoritative even where the visibility metric accepts alternatives.

The corpus reuses `SeedLibrarySearchFixture` and its 33 exact ranking regression
queries. The quality-only extension adds 12 Tracks covering every allowlisted
version, an ordinary parenthetical, `Live Forever`, and the eight-character typo
boundary; five Playlists cover Turkish I variants and non-Latin letters/digits.
Total quality fixture: 46 Tracks, 14 Albums, 16 Artists, 3 Genres, 7 Playlists
(including the deliberately inaccessible other-user Playlist). Credits and Genres
use existing normalized relationships. Original display names remain intact.

Primary seam: `OpenMigratedDB` creates a real temporary on-disk SQLite database,
applying migrations through version 37. Production Library Search routes execute
through Chi and HTTP request/response recorders. Every successful quality search
is validated against the OpenAPI response contract by `ServeContractRequest`.
There are no mocked search results, private-ranker assertions, alternate search
normalizers, or SQL-result oracles. HTTP is in-process, not a network/browser
latency measurement.

Visibility is Best Match **or the first five entries of the target's category**,
not the first five flattened dialog rows. Best Match must also lead its own
category and consume one of those five slots. Typed identities prevent accidental
matches between unrelated entity types with the same ID. A small evaluator check
covers the fifth/sixth category positions, Best Match, type collisions, and
alternative acceptable targets.

## Hard regressions and lifecycle evidence

The 33 `LibrarySearchRankingCases` deliberately update exact Best Match and all-group
identity/order expectations, including `weeknd blinding lights`, reordered words,
`blue`, `blue m`, `blue live`, `u`, `un`, `blidning lights`, `weknd blind`,
`blidning lihgts`, `someday`, `sxmeday`, edit thresholds, `sunflowar`, faithful
Beyoncé/Beyonce ordering, `a b`, `lights`, `echo`, and deterministic duplicate-title
ties. Credit-only Tracks/Albums and relationship expansions now have explicit
empty expectations; Best Match is included first in its category. The quality
matrix does not replace or weaken this exact oracle.

The hard-boundary suite verifies:

- All three relevance classes in one response; credit-only candidates are
  excluded and alphabetical secondary evidence cannot cross a class boundary.
- A strong Track, Album, Artist, Genre, or Playlist suppresses typo-only candidates
  in every group (five independent gate scenarios).
- Typo-only Tracks get corrected labels, no Best Match, no related expansion;
  related evidence does not prevent fallback.
- Exact Album precedes weaker direct Album; the Track's nonmatching Album is absent.
- Each category includes only independently eligible results, capped at five;
  Best Match is first in its own category and consumes one slot.
- `ar` finds Ariana Grande, not Bang Bang from its credit or Taylor Swift through
  its Album. Exact and corrected Artist-only queries exclude credit-only Tracks;
  mixed title-and-Artist queries remain valid, including `arinaa bang`.
- Playlist contents and Radio do not create library results.
- Version preference never crosses exact-name class precedence.
- Every allowlisted version beats an ordinary title containing its keyword within
  the same class; an unknown parenthetical remains ordinary searchable text.
- Repeated searches preserve full response identities/order for alphabetical,
  Artist/Album, stable-ID, and fuzzy ties.
- Per-word lengths 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, and 9; insertion, deletion, substitution,
  transposition; per-word and whole-query budget exclusions.

Every matrix response independently checks Best Match's direct label and first
category position, no repeated identity within categories, category membership,
five-entry group caps, corrected-only fallback labels, no fuzzy supplementation
of strong results, and no relationship-only results. `echo` fixes the exact five
Track identities including Best Match; no related Album or Artist is returned.
Existing HTTP tests cover original display metadata, normalized credits/Genres,
empty/whitespace/punctuation-only/overlong/missing queries, search failures, and
name/pending/deletion mutations.

`TestLibrarySearchQualityImportReplacementDeletion` reuses `StrictMP3Fixture`
and the real media inspector. HTTP upload preview remains unsearchable; confirmed
import becomes searchable by title combined with structured Track credits; confirmed
replacement preserves Track identity, updates folded and original display
credits, and removes old-credit results. An injected search-preparation write
failure returns an HTTP error while the previous searchable credit remains
intact. Confirmed deletion removes the Track and its now-unreferenced Album,
Artists, and Genres from search. No lifecycle result is inferred by reading SQL.
